Why it matters

1 in 4 adults

About 1 in 4 US adults has a disability, according to the CDC. Many of them are deciding where to book right now.

Everyone over 40

Low-contrast text is hard for older eyes and for anyone reading a phone outside. If they can't read your "Book now" button, some of them just don't book.

Fewer complaints

These are the same issues that show up most in website accessibility complaints. Fixing them now is simpler than dealing with one later.

What a fix looks like

Hard-to-read text (color contrast). Your brand purple #947cb4 is used behind white text in 16 spots, at a contrast of 3.1 to 1 (the standard minimum is 4.5).

Fix: change it to #776491 in your theme's color settings. One change fixes all 16 spots and keeps your look.
